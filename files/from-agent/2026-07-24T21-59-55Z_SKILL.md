## Full thesis text

# forge-organism · v5
### The Portal, the Taxonomy, and the Boundary Audit

*Supersedes v1–v4. Adds the two disciplines that make the rest safe.*

---

## 1. THE PORTAL — one currency through the gate

Everything TUNGSTEN knows funnels into a single comparison: **score against threshold.** Fourteen layers, nine regimes, six trims, six calibration phases, the entire organism apparatus — all of it exists to produce one number and compare it to another. If that comparison is inconsistent, everything upstream is wasted.

It is currently inconsistent in three ways, all silent.

### Failure 1 — the trims shrink the score, not the threshold

Six multiplicative trims, each ≤ 1.0, compound to **0.711**. The score loses 29% of its range.

| Threshold learned on raw scores (80th pct) | 74.0 |
|---|---|
| Trades expected | 20.0% |
| **Trades actually taken** | **0.1%** |

**99% of intended trades vanish.** Nothing errors. Nothing logs. The system simply becomes far more selective than anyone configured, and it looks exactly like *"no setups qualify."*

**→ Audit action: determine whether the conviction threshold was calibrated before or after the trim chain. If before, this bug is live.**

### Failure 2 — a score that ranks correctly is useless for sizing

| Raw score | True P(win) | Uncalibrated score claims | Kelly sizing error |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55 | 0.365 | 0.590 | **+366%** |
| 60 | 0.500 | 0.680 | +91% |
| 70 | 0.752 | 0.860 | +24% |

Ranking survives miscalibration. Sizing does not. Kelly consumes a probability; feed it a composite and it oversizes catastrophically at the very setups it is most confident about.

### Failure 3 — one global threshold cannot serve nine regimes

| Regime | Mean score | SD | % over global threshold 70 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | 56 | 7 | **2.2%** |
| Normal | 62 | 12 | 24.6% |
| Volatile | 68 | 20 | **45.4%** |

The same number means "top 2%" and "top 45%" depending only on the score distribution. Either normalise the score per regime or learn the threshold per regime — never leave both global.

### The fix — probability is the only currency

```
score      :=  P(win | all evidence)          calibrated, dimensionless
threshold  :=  (1 + cost) / (1 + RR − cost)   derived, not learned
```

At RR 2.30 with 0.05R costs: **threshold = 0.3231.**

Every downstream question is then answered by the same number in the same units:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Trade? | `P > threshold + margin` |
| How large? | `Kelly = (P(RR+1) − 1) / RR` |
| Abstain? | lower bound of CI on `P` falls below threshold |
| Trim? | adjust `P` **in probability units** — 6% trim means 6% less likely to win |
| Validate? | reliability curve: when it said 0.70, was it 0.70? |

**Why this is the unification and not merely a refactor.** A composite score cannot be checked against reality; a probability can. The reliability curve — built, and currently reporting into nothing — becomes the instrument that continuously audits the portal. Lean is a probability. Win rate is a probability. Break-even is a probability. Kelly consumes a probability. The score is the *only* object in the chain measured in arbitrary units, which is precisely why it needs a learned threshold to translate it.

**Convert it once and the translation layer disappears** — along with every drift and conflict that layer can host.

**Portal law:** *no quantity crosses the gate except a probability. Any component that cannot express itself in probability units does not get a vote.*

---

## 2. THE TAXONOMY — every defect ever found, classified

You asked that nothing like the portal failures ever exist again. "Scrutinise all 58,860 lines" is not achievable. But every defect this project has found — **all seventeen, including my own** — falls into eleven classes. Coverage is 100%.

| Class | Instances | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Conditioning mismatch** | 3 | 77% passed unchecked because a thin sample disabled its sibling checks; 20-bar exit vs validated per-regime horizon; one global threshold, nine regimes |
| **State mismatch** | 2 | champion never persisted across restart; eight version banners printing a stale build |
| **Unit mismatch** | 2 | break-even buffer in raw points = half a pip on 5-digit; composite score used where a probability is required |
| **Duplicated truth** | 2 | three hardcoded SL/TP brackets unaware of each other; 0.35/0.65 literals duplicating named constants |
| **Denominator mismatch** | 2 | policies compared per-trade not per-bar; no time cost on longer trades |
| **Fabricated value** | 1 | hardcoded 65.0 returned after optimisation |
| **Temporal mismatch** | 1 | stale PBO estimate leaking into a later run |
| **Sample mismatch** | 1 | live weights overwritable from 12 bars |
| **Orphaned output** | 1 | Deflated Sharpe computed for five days, consumed by nothing |
| **Scale mismatch** | 1 | threshold learned pre-trim, applied post-trim |
| **Estimation mismatch** | 1 | sizing from the true edge rather than the estimated one |

Nine of these were found in TUNGSTEN. Three were found in **my own charter**, by the same method. The taxonomy is not theoretical.

---

## 3. THE BOUNDARY AUDIT — how scrutiny becomes tractable

Defects do not live in lines. **They live where two quantities meet.** Comparisons, assignments across subsystems, function boundaries, persistence points. That set is orders of magnitude smaller than the codebase and it contains every defect above.

**At every boundary, eleven questions:**

1. **Units** — are both sides the same kind of thing? (probability vs score vs ATR vs bars vs points)
2. **Scale** — were both computed at the same point in the pipeline? (pre-trim vs post-trim)
3. **Conditioning** — were both computed under the same conditions? (global vs per-regime vs per-organism)
4. **Denominator** — per what? (per trade vs per bar vs per opportunity)
5. **Estimation** — is this the true value or an estimate? If estimated, where is its `n` and SE?
6. **Sample** — is `n` sufficient for the consequence this value carries?
7. **Temporal** — can this value outlive the run that produced it? Where is it invalidated?
8. **State** — does it survive a restart? Should it?
9. **Duplication** — is this truth stated anywhere else? If so, which is canonical?
10. **Consumption** — is this output actually read? By whom?
11. **Failure mode** — if the input is missing, does it fail open at neutral, or guess?

**Enforcement, not aspiration:**

- **Every numeric quantity carries a type tag** — `PROB`, `ATR`, `BARS`, `POINTS`, `RATIO`, `CURRENCY`. Comparing unlike tags is a defect by definition, detectable by grep rather than by insight.
- **Every estimate travels as `(value, n, SE, prior, shrinkage)`.** A bare number may not cross a subsystem boundary.
- **Every persisted value declares its invalidation point.** No value without a stated death.
- **Every computed output declares its consumer.** An output with no consumer is either wired in or deleted — never left computing quietly for five days.
- **Every canonical truth exists once.** A literal duplicating a named constant is a defect even when the values match, because they will not match forever.
- **Every pass runs the boundary audit on what the previous pass changed** before adding anything new.

**Honest limit.** A taxonomy catches known classes. New classes will appear — and when one does, the correct response is to add a twelfth row and re-audit, not to conclude the list was complete. **The value is not that eleven is the right number. It is that the number is small enough to check every time.**

---

## 4. Retained: the Law of Interior Optima

> Every parameter has a middle that beats both extremes. A setting at a boundary is a default nobody tested.

| Parameter | Low | **Optimum** | High | Current | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reward : risk | 1.2 → 15.1 | **2.0 → 38.1** | 3.5 → 25.1 | 2.30 | near-optimal |
| Abstention gate | 0.0 → 27.7 | **0.5 → 39.8** | 0.8 → 23.7 | **none** | **the one real gain** |
| Kelly fraction | 0.10 → 15.4 | **0.35–0.50** | 0.75+ unstable | 0.25 | defensible |
| Trailing slack | — | not resolvable | — | cell MAE | keep; never optimise |
| Shrinkage k | — | 25 | — | 25 | keep |

Mechanism is identical in each case: **two opposing costs that cross.** Wider targets win bigger but take longer — trades per year fell from 1,180 at 1.2R to 453 at 3.5R.

**Every optimum is a band. Ship the middle of the plateau, never the peak.**

**Tune in coupled groups.** RR sets break-even → sets lean threshold → sets the gate. Kelly and abstention both control risk; tightening both double-counts. Slack and hazard both end trades; misalign them and one silently disables the other.

---

## 5. Retained: the reasoning layer

- Never a raw estimate — shrink toward a hierarchical prior. 81% error reduction at n=5, 12% at n=200, **never worse**.
- Borrow from neighbours for thin types — dominates at every sample size.
- **Abstain on sign uncertainty, not magnitude uncertainty.** Direction error costs 2.8 per trade.
- Prefer the simpler estimator — a tally beat weight optimisation at every sample size to 4,000.

---

## 6. Charter — sequencing

1. **Run the boundary audit on the existing portal first.** Specifically: was the conviction threshold calibrated pre- or post-trim? This gates everything, because a broken portal makes all downstream measurement meaningless.
2. **Convert the score to a calibrated probability. Derive the threshold.**
3. **Measure lean with uncertainty.** Per organism type: instances, life, lean, SE, `P(lean > threshold)`.
   **Falsification gate:** no type clearing threshold with ≥100 instances out-of-sample → **stop and report.**
4. **Install the reasoning layer** before anything consumes estimates.
5. **Measure execution cost** — sets the threshold.
6. **Measure independence; apply `quorum = ceil(0.67 × n/(1+(n−1)ρ̄))`.**
7. **Install the abstention gate at 0.50.**
8. **Re-verify RR, Kelly and gate as a coupled group.**
9. **Ghost mode as the instance factory** — zero-risk manufacture of the scarce resource.
10. **Re-index the probability bank** by `(organism type, lifecycle stage, quorum count)`.
11. **Trail + hazard exit.** Structure-break on entry only. Slack from measured MAE, never optimised.

**Standing constraints**

- **Portal law** — probability is the only currency through the gate.
- **Boundary audit** — eleven questions at every junction, every pass.
- **Interior optima** — no setting at a boundary without a test justifying it.
- **Every optimum is a band**; ship the middle.
- **Type tags on every quantity.** Unlike tags compared = defect.
- **No bare point estimates.** No value without a stated death. No output without a consumer. No truth stated twice.
- Deflated Sharpe gates every organism definition tested.
- ≥100 instances before any per-type figure touches a live decision.
- Per-run consent on live-decision changes. Unchanged.
- **Check the denominator.** Three of my own flaws were denominator errors.
- **Every pass audits the previous pass before adding to it.** All twenty defects to date were found this way — none by reasoning.

---

## 7. The ratchet

```
SCORE = P(lean > threshold) × log-growth-per-bar × instance-count credit
```

Retained only if SCORE improves on data unseen when the change was made, by more than SE(SCORE). Otherwise reverted.

**Convergence:** mean gain over 5 passes < 1×SE → warn. Over 10 passes < 0.5×SE → **stop and report.**

**Mandatory stops:** falsification fails; three consecutive passes ship nothing; any gain not attributable to a specific change.

**A ratchet that never flattens is not converging — it is overfitting.**

---

## 8. Open flaws, declared

- **Entry/exit interaction untested.** Each optimised holding the other fixed; the joint optimum likely differs.
- **Organism-type drift unhandled.** The charter assumes a type's lean is stable across years. This is the non-stationarity problem one level up — the same trap that produced five months of nulls.
- **Sweep noise.** One Kelly value returned far below both neighbours. Sampling error, not signal. Every optimum is provisional.
- **The taxonomy is incomplete by construction.** Eleven classes cover everything found so far. Class twelve exists and has not yet been met.
- **Simulation is not evidence about your market.**

---

## 9. The one measurement

**`P(lean > 0.52)` for your best organism type, out of sample, with measured costs.**

| Result | Verdict |
|---|---|
| ≥ 0.95, ≥100 instances | Strong. The nulls were an artefact of pooling. Build everything. |
| 0.90 – 0.95 | Workable. Full charter proceeds. |
| 0.75 – 0.90 | Marginal. Viable only with the abstention gate and long-lived organisms. |
| < 0.75 | **Dead.** Organisms do not sort by direction. Stop; reconsider the timeframe. |

No live terminal required. Computable today from history on disk.

**The advantages, stacked:** a portal that becomes self-auditing the moment it speaks in probability. An abstention gate worth +44% log growth that does not yet exist. Ghost mode manufacturing the scarce resource for free. Shrinkage cutting estimation error up to 81% and never worse. RR and Kelly already near their optima after five months of careful work. And a taxonomy small enough to run at every boundary, every pass, forever.

Each is small. They multiply rather than add, and compound on every trade for as long as the system runs — **provided the lean is real.** That remains unmeasured, and it is the only thing that decides whether any of this is worth building.

Five months searched for a weighting that predicts returns — implicitly measuring a 50/50 mixture whose answer is zero by construction. It returned exactly that, 562,500 times.

**The question was wrong, not the machine.**

   against the actual current `ComputeEntryConviction` trim chain and wherever the threshold itself

2. Only proceed to §6 step 2+ (probability conversion, per-organism-type lean measurement, etc.) if
   step 1 finds a real, confirmed defect, or if Manuel explicitly wants the broader probability-

   → report, one bounded change at a time, never a giant unreviewable batch.
