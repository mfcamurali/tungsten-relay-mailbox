=== METHODOLOGICAL FINDINGS (about the watch mechanism itself, not the EA) ===
1. TUNGSTEN_live.log is OVERWRITTEN per TLog() call, not appended. Two TLog calls landing inside the same 5-second poll window means the earlier one is silently lost to any external watcher, even though it genuinely executed. Explains why some stages (e.g. r2's ascent-200) never appeared in the feed.
2. TLog's embedded timestamp reflects MQL4's TimeCurrent() (last-known SERVER TICK time), not wall-clock write time. A CPU-bound loop (e.g. 30,000 draws) faster than a new tick arrives will stamp multiple log lines with the identical embedded time even though real elapsed time clearly passed. Use the watcher's own "(written ...)" wall-clock stamp for true timing, not the embedded string.
