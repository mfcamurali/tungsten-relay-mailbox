## C1.1 — the root cause is NOT the mutations

The prose said 54 mutations. The real count is **45**. But they are not the cause.

CEUS ends with a rescale its twin has no counterpart for:
`_norm = totalScore * (88.0 / _refMax)` — at the weights in force, **88/169 = 0.5207**.

Counterfactuals over 49,999 exported bars (mean |gap|):

| scenario | gap |
|---|---|
| as shipped | 18.36 |
| twin applies all 45 mutations | **18.99 — worse** |
| twin applies the rescale | **4.31 — −77%** |

Applying the mutations makes it worse because they push *with* the signal (mean +0.94) while the
rescale shrinks it — they were partially cancelling. K is identified from the data, not assumed:
6.45% of bars land exactly on one of four ratios K×{0.82, 1.00, 1.10, 1.25} against 0.96% by chance.

**This is the trade-suppression mechanism.** Calibration fits thresholds on the twin's scale; live
applies them to CEUS's half-scale output. At signed threshold 55 the twin qualifies 26.5% of bars
and live qualifies 1.3% — reproducing the portal audit's 132-vs-1 from the scale factor alone. The
build's own portal audit has been **logging that ratio** (`mean |enhanced|/|linear| 0.616`) on the
same line as the collapse for a month without naming it.

**Recommendation: extract CEUS's tail into a shared helper both call, behind
`ScorerIdentityNormalise` defaulting OFF** (the WARRANT#226 pattern, ~25 lines). It **invalidates
every stored threshold** — they were fitted on the unnormalised scale and are wrong by ~1.9×
**toward trading more** — so it cannot ship without a recalibration. **Not shipped. This one is
yours.**
